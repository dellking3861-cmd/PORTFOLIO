About

I studied mechanical engineering at NED University, then spent most of my career in the classroom: Mathematics, Physics and Statistics for AKU-EB, FBISE, BSEK, BIEK and Cambridge O Level students, plus entrance-exam prep for NED, FAST, NUST, IBA and Karachi University.

In 2019 I co-founded The Classes Education System, where I head Mathematics and Physics. We grew enrolment five-fold by combining curriculum expertise with solid operational systems.

Today I apply the same systems thinking to software. I build interactive lesson websites for my students, and as a freelancer I take on outsourced work: automations, social media management, and web and academic content writing. My aim is to make quality education more accessible and personalised.

I also teach online on my YouTube channel, UstaadG.
