About

I studied mechanical engineering at NED University, then spent most of my career in the classroom: Mathematics, Physics and Statistics for AKU-EB, FBISE, BSEK, BIEK and Cambridge O Level students, plus entrance-exam prep for NED, FAST, NUST, IBA and Karachi University.

In 2019 I co-founded The Classes Education System, where I head Mathematics and Physics. Running it taught me that good teaching needs good systems behind it. Scheduling, materials and tracking progress all take time away from students.

That is why I now build software. At KeieiSol I work on small SaaS products and automations: a CV screener, a medical chart transcriber, chatbots and voice agents. My goal is tools that give teachers time back and give students practice that fits where they are.

I also teach online on my YouTube channel, UstaadG, including a full Sindh Board Maths Class 9 & 10 series.
